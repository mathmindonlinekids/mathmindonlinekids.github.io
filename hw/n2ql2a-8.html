Remember

Hundreds: 1,400 − 600 → 14 hundreds − 6 hundreds = 8 hundreds = 800. Hard one? Two jumps: 900 − 450 → 900 − 400 = 500 → 500 − 50 = 450.
Rounding: look at the digit to the right. 5 or more → round up. 4 or less → round down.
Part or whole? We know the whole → divide (part). We know one part → multiply (whole). Draw a stick!
400 − y = 150: 400 is the whole. y and 150 are the parts. Missing part = whole − part: y = 400 − 150 = 250.
Two steps: go backwards and undo the last step first.
