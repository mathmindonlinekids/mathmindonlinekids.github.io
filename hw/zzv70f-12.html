Day 1 · Tuesday

Day 1

1. Tricky Warm-up

Read every word. Each question hides a trap. Draw a picture if it helps!

2. Brackets and Equations

Multiply every number inside the brackets: 3 × (x + 4) = 3x + 12 and 3 × (x − 4) = 3x − 12.
Minus before brackets: 20 − (8 + 5) = 20 − 8 − 5, but 20 − (8 − 5) = 20 − 8 + 5.
Equation with brackets: open the brackets → undo + or − → undo ×. Check: put your x back in!

3. Lines and Angles

Parallel: never meet. Perpendicular: meet at a right angle. A right angle is marked with a little square. No square? Test it with the corner of a sheet of paper.
Letters: look at every pair of straight parts. Some pairs can be parallel and other pairs perpendicular, so the answer can be “both”.
