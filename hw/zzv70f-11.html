Day 1

1. Tricky Warm-up

Read every word. Each question hides a trap. Draw a picture if it helps!

2. Spiral: Open the Brackets

Multiply every number inside the brackets: 3 × (x + 4) = 3x + 12 and 3 × (x − 4) = 3x − 12.
Minus before brackets: 20 − (8 + 5) = 20 − 8 − 5, but 20 − (8 − 5) = 20 − 8 + 5.

3. Lines and Angles

Parallel: never meet. Perpendicular: meet at a right angle. Acute < right angle < obtuse. Use the corner of a sheet of paper as your right-angle tester!
