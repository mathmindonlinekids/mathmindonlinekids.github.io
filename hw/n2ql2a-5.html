1. Mental Math: Round Numbers

Think in tens and hundreds: 40 + 50 → 4 tens + 5 tens = 9 tens = 90. 300 + 400 → 3 hundreds + 4 hundreds = 700.
⭐ Number trains. Start with the first number. Do each step in your head and write the new number.

2. Times Table: 6, 7, 8, 9

Stuck? Use a fact you know: 6 × 8 = 5 × 8 + 8 = 40 + 8 = 48.

3. Share Equally

Divide = share equally. 28 ÷ 4 = 7, because 4 × 7 = 28.

4. Part of a Number

How to read a fraction: 23 (two thirds).
The bottom number 3 = split the whole into 3 equal parts.
The top number 2 = take 2 of those parts.
First find 1 part (divide), then take as many parts as the top number says.

5. Find the Whole

We know a part. 14 (one fourth) = 1 part of 4. If 1 part is 8, the whole is 4 parts: 4 × 8 = 32. Draw a stick to help!

6. Subtract with Regrouping

Not enough ones or tens? Borrow from the next place. A zero? Borrow from the hundreds first: 500 = 4 hundreds 9 tens 10 ones. Check with addition!

7. Time Across the Hour

1 hour = 60 minutes. Jump to the full hour first, then add the rest.

8. Area and Perimeter

Perimeter = add all the sides. Area = count the squares (length × width).

9. I Thought of a Number

Go backwards and undo: added → take away; took away → add; multiplied → divide.
