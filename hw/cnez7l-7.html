3. Divisibility Rules

÷ 10: last digit 0. ÷ 5: last digit 0 or 5. ÷ 3: the sum of the digits divides by 3. ÷ 4: the last two digits divide by 4.

4. Story to Math

Read carefully: who has more? “A dress costs 4 times as much as a hat” means the hat is the smaller one: 4 hats = 1 dress, so hat = dress ÷ 4. Draw a bar if you are not sure!

5. Equations with Brackets

① Open the brackets: 3(x + 1) = 3x + 3. ② Letters on both sides? Take away the same letters from both sides: 3x + 3 = x + 13 → 2x + 3 = 13. ③ Remove the number, divide. ④ Check!

6. Prime Factors

Break the number into prime numbers only (2, 3, 5, 7, 11…). Use the divisibility rules to find a factor fast!

7. Angles

Acute < 90°, right = 90°, obtuse between 90° and 180°, straight = 180°, reflex > 180°. A full turn = 360°.
