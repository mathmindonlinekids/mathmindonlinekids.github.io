1. Warm-up: Facts to 12

Say the answer fast, then type it.

2. Story to Math: Bigger or Smaller?

Find “… times as much as …”. The thing before “times” is the bigger one.
Ask: Who is bigger? Do I know the bigger one or the smaller one?
Know the big one → ÷. Know the small one → ×. Draw two bars if you are not sure!

3. Angles

Protractor: center on the vertex, one side on 0, read where the other side crosses. Small ticks are every 5°.
Right angle = 90°. Straight angle = 180°. Full turn = 360°. Check every answer by adding!

4. Review: One of Each

÷ 6: even AND the digit sum divides by 3. ÷ 4: the last two digits divide by 4. Equations: open the brackets, take away the same letters from both sides, check!

5. Prime Factors from Every Side

“Prime factorization”, “write as a product of primes”, “break into primes” all mean the same thing: write the number as primes multiplied together (2, 3, 5, 7, 11…). Example: 12 = 2 × 2 × 3.

6. Fractions

The bottom number (denominator) = how many equal parts in the whole. The top number (numerator) = how many parts we take. Equal fractions: multiply top and bottom by the same number: 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6.
