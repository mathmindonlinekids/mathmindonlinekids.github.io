Prime Factors: Same Question, New Words

21–26 min
“Prime factorization of 20”, “write 20 as a product of primes”, “which primes multiply to make 20?”, “break 20 into primes” — all the same question! Answer: 20 = 2 × 2 × 5.

NEW: Money with Dollars and Cents

26–34 min
$1 = 100¢. A quarter = 25¢, a dime = 10¢, a nickel = 5¢, a penny = 1¢.
$3.45 means 3 dollars and 45 cents. Line up the dots to add or subtract: dollars under dollars, cents under cents.
Change = money you give − price.

NEW: Feet and Inches

34–42 min
1 foot = 12 inches. 1 yard = 3 feet.
Big unit → small unit: × 12 (more pieces). Small unit → big unit: ÷ 12, and the remainder stays in inches.

NEW: Time: Hours, Minutes, Seconds

42–52 min
1 hour = 60 minutes. 1 minute = 60 seconds. 1 day = 24 hours.
Elapsed time: jump on a time line — first to the next full hour, then the rest. 12:00 is where a.m. changes to p.m.
