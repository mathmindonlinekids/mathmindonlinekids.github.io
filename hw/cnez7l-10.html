Remember

Fraction: the bottom number (denominator) = how many equal parts in the whole. The top number (numerator) = how many parts we take.
Part of a number: first find one part (÷ bottom), then take the parts you need (× top). 3/4 of 20: one part = 20 ÷ 4 = 5, three parts = 3 × 5 = 15.
Equal fractions: multiply (or divide) the top and the bottom by the same number. 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8.
