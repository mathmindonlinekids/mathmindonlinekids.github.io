Day 1 · Sunday

Mental math

Think in hundreds: 1,400 − 600 → 14 hundreds − 6 hundreds = 8 hundreds = 800. 900 − 450 → 900 − 400 = 500, then − 50 = 450.

Part or whole?

Part: we know the whole number → divide. Whole: we know one part → multiply. First decide which one!

Shapes

Ask 3 questions: Right angles? Equal sides? Parallel sides?

Estimate, then solve

Round each number to the nearest ten, solve in your head. Then solve exactly. Are the answers close?

I thought of a number

Go backwards: undo each step. × → ÷, + → −, − → +.
