1. Warm-up: Tens and Magic Pairs

7 × 40 = 7 × 4 tens = 28 tens = 280. Find a magic pair (10, 100, 1,000) first.

2. Missing Factor

Division is multiplication with a missing factor: 63 ÷ 9 = □ means 9 × □ = 63.

3. Number Patterns

Find the rule: what changes each time? Odd × odd = odd. If one factor is even, the product is even.

4. Add and Subtract within 1,000

Line up hundreds, tens, ones. When the top digit is 0, borrow from the next place: 700 = 6 hundreds + 9 tens + 10 ones. Check subtraction with addition!
