1. Warm-up: Tens and Hundreds

If 32 ÷ 4 = 8, then 320 ÷ 4 = 8 tens = 80.

2. Divide in Parts

Split the number into parts that are easy to divide (hundreds, tens…). Divide each part, then add. Find the parts yourself on paper! Check with ×.

3. Divide with a Remainder

Divide in parts. What is left is the remainder. It must be smaller than the number you divide by.

4. Estimate First

Use a near number that is easy to divide: 489 ÷ 7 ≈ 490 ÷ 7 = 70.

5. Story to Math

For every story: ① What do I know? ② What do I need to find? ③ Which action: put together (+), take away / compare (−), equal groups or “times as many” (×), share equally or “how many groups” (÷)? ④ Choose the sign, then solve.

6. Two-Step Stories

Find the first step (what you need to know first), then the answer. Draw a picture or a bar if it helps!

7. Equations

① Divide or open the brackets ② remove the number ③ divide ④ check.
