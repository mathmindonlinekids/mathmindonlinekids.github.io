1. Warm-up: Magic Pairs

Find a pair that makes 10, 100 or 1,000 first: 2 × 5 = 10, 4 × 25 = 100, 8 × 125 = 1,000. You can split a number to make a pair: 24 × 25 = 6 × 4 × 25.

2. Area with a Mystery Side

Area = length × width. A letter stands for a number we do not know yet. Write the equation first: 9 × M = 63.

3. Word Problems: Parts in Words

Draw a bar with equal parts. One third = 1 of 3 equal parts. One fourth = 1 of 4 equal parts. Read carefully: do they ask for the part, or for the rest?
